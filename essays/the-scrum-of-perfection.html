The Scrum of Perfection

Life is a scrum, an unfair – odds stacked – guaranteed to lose – struggle….and from the grime and blood and dung arise the Light of Beauty and glimpses of Love.
Look not for perfection, in anyone or anything or yourself. Look for the moments of Light, the flash of eternity, and celebrate your role in creating Love made crystalline perfect by the imperfections of this world and its people through which that Love is filtered, refined, and distilled.
