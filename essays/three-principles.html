Three Principles

Were we to boil everything down to three principles:

  • Unity, meaning the strengthening of all individuals as individuals, and the strengthening of the bonds among individuals, is the overarching calling, direction, objective, and joy for which we (unknowingly) strive in this life and are destined in eternal Life.
  • Genuine spiritual practice and authentic worship ultimately are actualized in how you treat all living forms and beings placed before you.
  • The Creative force of Love, called Christos by some, underlies all existence. You are not alone.
