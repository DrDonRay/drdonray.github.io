The Inevitable and the Certain

Filed only under Foundation – Purpose – Meaning – Destiny

In this world the balance required to maintain the Choice (necessary for the creation of pure Love) means that that which is holy and pure will inevitably come to disaster.

Worldly opportunities will appear, seemingly offering a chance for the holy and pure to take root and flourish. But inevitably the world tears the unfitting holy asunder, the best-appearing opportunity proving to be yet another mirage, the corrupt and shattered nature of this world again ignoring or distorting or destroying Truth and its Source.

Yet still the opportunities must be taken, illusory and doomed as they are. For the temporal balance that assures the pure and holy will be sullied also assures the Light will not be extinguished. The holy may dissipate, but it also permeates. Hope is momentary, but also eternal. Crucifixion in its many guises may be inevitable, but Resurrection in its many forms is a certainty.
