Save the World a Little Later

Peace. It is OK to take a moment off.

Peace. Peace. Let the healing happen.

Calm. Wonder.

This shall remain a mystery a while longer.

There is enough to keep track of. It is all changing.

There will be plenty of time for boldness and courage. Plenty of opportunities for everyone.

Keep listening. Then listen some more. The answers are not intrusive or forceful.

There is so far to grow and so much to learn. That must always be the case.

Of course it makes no sense. So it’s OK for a change to relax and soak up the healing.

Such an amazing path brought you this far!

This is a critical turning point in the world.

Peace, peace, “fear not” if you can believe that.

Calm, peace, no need to look deeply.

Let the security envelop you, a warm cushly blanket, all that love of the ages allowed entry, no longer hidden. That is an adequate answer for now, and, truth be known, it is the ultimate answer. All the other analysis and compartmentalization is just necessary distraction. For a moment allow consciousness to shake off all the thoughts and models and understanding and let the soul be touched and cuddled and wrapped in warm security.

Even the body’s discomforts abate a little in this state of awareness.

Peace, peace, blessed peace. The grandiose lessons can wait a little longer. The profound insights are, after all, just ways to reach the peace and the warm cushly blanket state of being.

The world will still be waiting out there. It can still be saved a little later.

For now

Peace

Peace,

A flowing security.

There is enough to do that a moment not doing is OK.

Take the Peace with you back into the world. You will need it. Others will need it.

The Peace brings healing. The Peace awaits after time, after all. It is OK to fast-forward to it for a moment.

Allow the embrace of Yeshu and the cloud of saints and the warm gifts of touch.

Peace, peace, penetrating so deeply that you will never quite completely lose it.
