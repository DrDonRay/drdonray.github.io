Shock Prayer

Filed only under Naming the Spiritual

God, help us.
God, shock us.
Shock us out of complacency and the status quo.
Don’t let us slip away from any lessons without learning them.
Don’t let us finish any day without changing and growing in your Spirit.
Amen.
