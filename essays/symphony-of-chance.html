Symphony of Chance

Hear the chimes, that penetrating sound, the sound of wind instantiated. Harmonious, ethereal sound released by impact                 and collision. Hear the chimes, allow their ringing to                 penetrate, a purity of frequency from which to build                 a world. Let others wrestle with                 meanings obtuse and obvious. Hear the chimes,                 sounds soothing,                 sounds alarming,                 sounds announcing                             arrivals. The chimes announce Choices, speaking of change, heralding growth. Beautiful, beautiful,                 fading as soon as struck, the chimes call to awareness                 and remembrance, Each tone struck of clashing                 forms soon lost to time. Countless tones,                 weaving, intermingled so soon fading and gone. No song or composition                 perform these chimes, just each a note, each played by chance, to penetrate these times. We look for patterns, repeating lines, some theme or structured                 cadence.  Surely life must be so, each note a part of whole. But no, the beauty of                 each chiming tone is much more pure than that, perfection in the tone itself, and its harmony with others. Therein lies melodious Source, each tone the full investment. The rhythms imagined, the patterns random, the composition pure. Hear the beauty, sense perfection, built from sum of                 individuals. Hear the composition                 of swirling winds,                 never to be repeated.                 Trust it, let it be,                 this symphony of chance.
