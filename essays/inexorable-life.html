Inexorable Life

Ultimately, what is Life? Life is the creation of our essence, we the children of God.

Life, our lives, creation of our essence, will happen as surely as a river flowing downhill.

Our choices may occasionally block the flow, but inevitably life will break through and resume the flow.

Our choices set the specific course details, but the flow will happen one way or another.

We should seek to optimize our growth/flow with a balance of discipline and free exploration. Extremes illustrate how we stop the flow of life and growth of soul, the extreme of unyielding regimen and restraint, the extreme of profligate indulgence in every temptation, both extremes stifling and crushing the life experience of the soul, though one might produce a sculpted body and the other a destroyed body.

The life flow and growth will inevitably happen as long as we don’t hew to extreme collapse into the little self, illustrated in both preceding examples. There are therefore usually no perfectly right or wrong choices. Life and its Source are far more generous and forgiving and creative than that.

So live, try, experience, grow, learn, in so doing fulfilling the purpose of your existence. Do it in continual loving expansion into greater Unity, each choice and decision guided by Love of Source and all living things. That is the way to follow the firstborn into our full potential.
