You Will Be Touched

The human spirit, or is it soul, or do we have no name for it, something individual, something lost, or was it forgotten? Something survives in the face of it all, something of the God that we would deny. Something survives, inextinguishable, always nascent, a potential. Do not turn from it, you distant listener, you detached observer, you bored reader. This is about you. You have either survived too much or you will survive too much to deny that pricking at your unnamed soul. Flee for now if you must, but there is no escape. Consciousness will catch up to you. The whole Universe orbits about your life, waiting for you to look at something other than yourself. Uncomfortable, isn't it? Squirm a little. Maybe this isn't the first time you felt the touch of something trying to wake you. It's Love you know. Calling you to your potential. No hurry. You have all eternity to grow into it. Relax. You’re not to blame. No one is to blame. Your failings were all part of the learning. Their failings were all opportunities for forgiveness. Let it catch you unawares, clueless and lost, or maybe you think you have all the answers. Maybe you are in denial or secure in genuine peace. Maybe you are frightened of life or hardened to its threats. Maybe you pray for delivery or give thanks for it or curse the decider of the outcomes…. ….as if anyone or anything decided the outcomes. Yet something survives in the face of it all…. something of you…. something that expresses the Source that underlies you. For now go on your way. Think not too much about it, not that the world allows much chance for such thought. Know that you were momentarily touched, and you will be touched again, by Love, for lack of a better word. You will be touched, ever so gently touched, and someday you will be ready for it. No hurry. You have all eternity to grow into it.
